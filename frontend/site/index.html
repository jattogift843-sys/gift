How it works

  1. Open the AI Trading Bot tab in your member dashboard.
  2. Choose your plan — trading duration and profit target from the options MT5 Smart Market sets.
  3. Enter your stake. It moves from your available balance into the bot for the duration of the run.
  4. The AI trades automatically — roughly a trade every few minutes, biased toward your target.
  5. It finishes when the profit target is hit or the duration ends, whichever comes first.
  6. You're paid out — original stake plus all profit returns to your balance instantly.

Automated trading carries risk. Targets are objectives, not guarantees; the bot can never lose more than the stake you commit to a session.
